# 通用对话语言块

## 寒暄和近况

### 1. `How's it going?`

用于轻松地询问对方近况。

- Hey, how's it going?
- How's it going at your new job?
- I haven't seen you in a while. How's it going?

### 2. `What have you been up to?`

用于询问对方最近在忙什么、做了什么。

- What have you been up to lately?
- I haven't seen you in months. What have you been up to?
- What have you been up to since you changed jobs?

### 3. `How was ...?`

用于询问某次活动或一段经历怎么样。

- How was your weekend?
- How was work today?
- How was your trip to Shanghai?

### 4. `It's been a while.`

表示“好久不见、已经有一段时间了”。

- It's been a while. How have you been?
- It's been a while since we last talked.
- It's been a while since I visited this place.

## 邀请、安排和改期

### 5. `Are you free ...?`

用于询问对方某个时间是否有空。

- Are you free this weekend?
- Are you free for lunch tomorrow?
- Are you free after work?

### 6. `Would you like to ...?`

用于礼貌地邀请对方做某事。

- Would you like to join us for dinner?
- Would you like to get some coffee?
- Would you like to come over this weekend?

### 7. `Can we move it to ...?`

用于请求把已经安排好的事情改到另一个时间。

- Can we move it to Friday?
- Can we move it to tomorrow morning?
- Can we move it to sometime next week?

### 8. `Something came up.`

表示临时发生了意外情况，因此需要改变原来的安排。

- Something came up, so I can't make it tonight.
- I'm sorry, but something came up at work.
- Something came up. Can we meet another day?

### 9. `Maybe another time.`

用于委婉地拒绝邀请，同时保留以后再约的可能。

- I can't make it tonight. Maybe another time.
- I'm really busy this week, but maybe another time.
- Thanks for asking. Maybe another time.

## 同意、选择和拒绝

### 10. `I completely agree.`

用于明确表示完全同意。

- I completely agree with you.
- I completely agree that we need more time.
- That's exactly how I see it. I completely agree.

### 11. `That's a good point.`

用于认可对方提出的观点。

- That's a good point. I hadn't thought about that.
- That's a good point about the cost.
- You're right. That's a good point.

### 12. `I'm not sure about ...`

用于表达不确定、保留意见或轻微反对。

- I'm not sure about that idea.
- I'm not sure about changing the plan now.
- I'm not sure about the timing.

### 13. `I'm afraid I can't ...`

用于礼貌地拒绝请求或邀请。

- I'm afraid I can't make it tonight.
- I'm afraid I can't help you with that.
- I'm afraid I can't stay very long.

### 14. `I'd rather ...`

用于表达自己更愿意选择什么，后接动词原形。

- I'd rather stay home tonight.
- I'd rather talk about it in person.
- I'd rather wait until we have more information.

### 15. `Either one is fine with me.`

表示两个选择自己都可以接受。

- Morning or afternoon? Either one is fine with me.
- We can take the bus or the subway. Either one is fine with me.
- We can eat here or somewhere else. Either one is fine with me.

## 接话、打断和听不清

### 16. `Can I add something?`

用于礼貌地加入对话或补充观点。

- Can I add something?
- Can I add something to what you just said?
- Before we move on, can I add something?

### 17. `Sorry to interrupt, but ...`

用于不得不打断对方时先表示礼貌。

- Sorry to interrupt, but I have a quick question.
- Sorry to interrupt, but we're running out of time.
- Sorry to interrupt, but I think I missed something.

### 18. `Go ahead.`

用于请对方继续说、先说或先做。

- You wanted to say something? Go ahead.
- Go ahead. I'm listening.
- If you have a question, go ahead.

### 19. `Could you say that again?`

用于请对方重复刚才的话。

- Sorry, could you say that again?
- I didn't catch the last part. Could you say that again?
- Could you say that again a little more slowly?

### 20. `Could you speak a little more slowly?`

用于请对方放慢语速。

- Could you speak a little more slowly?
- I'm still learning English. Could you speak a little more slowly?
- Sorry, could you speak a little more slowly this time?

### 21. `Let me think about that.`

用于在回答问题前争取思考时间。

- Let me think about that for a second.
- That's an interesting question. Let me think about that.
- Let me think about that before I give you an answer.

## 说明原因、比较和结果

### 22. `The main reason is ...`

用于直接说明最主要的原因。

- The main reason is the cost.
- The main reason I left early was that I felt sick.
- The main reason we chose this option is its simplicity.

### 23. `That's why ...`

用于说明前面的情况导致了什么结果。

- I missed the bus. That's why I was late.
- The store was closed. That's why I came back empty-handed.
- We need more test data. That's why we haven't released it yet.

### 24. `The difference is that ...`

用于说明两件事之间最重要的区别。

- The difference is that this one is cheaper.
- The difference is that the old version works offline.
- The difference is that she works from home.

### 25. `Compared with ..., ...`

用于比较两个人、事物或情况。

- Compared with last week, I feel much better.
- Compared with the old version, this one is easier to use.
- Compared with driving, taking the subway is more convenient.

### 26. `For example, ...`

用于给前面的观点补充一个具体例子。

- Some tasks can be automated. For example, we can automate the tests.
- I like outdoor activities. For example, I often go hiking.
- There are several ways to get there. For example, you can take Line 2.

### 27. `In other words, ...`

用于换一种更清楚的方式重新说明。

- In other words, we need to start again.
- In other words, the change only affects new users.
- In other words, I won't be available until Monday.

### 28. `As a result, ...`

用于说明前面的事情造成的结果。

- The train was delayed. As a result, I arrived late.
- We removed the extra request. As a result, the page loads faster.
- He didn't sleep well. As a result, he felt tired all day.

## 总结和结束对话

### 29. `So, basically, ...`

用于用简单的话概括前面的内容，语气比较口语化。

- So, basically, we have two options.
- So, basically, I need to do the whole thing again.
- So, basically, nothing will change for current users.

### 30. `To sum up, ...`

用于在较正式的说明或讨论中总结重点。

- To sum up, we need more time and better test data.
- To sum up, the trip was tiring but enjoyable.
- To sum up, both options have advantages and disadvantages.

### 31. `That's all I wanted to say.`

用于说明自己已经表达完毕。

- That's all I wanted to say about the issue.
- I think I've covered everything. That's all I wanted to say.
- That's all I wanted to say for now.

### 32. `It was nice talking to you.`

用于礼貌地结束一次谈话。

- It was nice talking to you.
- It was nice talking to you after all this time.
- I have to leave now, but it was nice talking to you.

### 33. `I should get going.`

用于自然地表示自己该离开了。

- It's getting late. I should get going.
- I have an early meeting tomorrow, so I should get going.
- Thanks for dinner. I should get going.

### 34. `Let's talk again soon.`

用于结束谈话时表示希望以后继续联系。

- It was great catching up. Let's talk again soon.
- I have to go, but let's talk again soon.
- Let me know how things go, and let's talk again soon.
