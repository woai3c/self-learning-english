# 常用搭配

这一页收集可以放进句中或句尾的高频语言块，包括动词搭配、短语动词和
时间短语。它们不一定组成完整句子，需要和人物、时间或具体事情组合使用。

## 日常生活搭配

### 1. `make a mistake`

表示“犯错误”。根据语境可以使用 `make`、`made` 或 `makes`。

- I made a mistake in the message.
- Don't worry if you make a mistake.
- We all make mistakes from time to time.

### 2. `make a decision`

表示“作出决定”。

- I need more time to make a decision.
- Have you made a decision yet?
- We finally made a decision last night.

### 3. `make plans`

表示“制订计划、约好活动”。

- We haven't made any plans for the weekend.
- It's hard to make plans this far in advance.
- I made plans to have dinner with a friend.

### 4. `take a break`

表示“休息一下”。

- Let's take a short break.
- I need to take a break from the screen.
- We took a break after lunch.

### 5. `take your time`

表示“不用着急、慢慢来”。人称可以根据对象变化。

- Take your time. There's no rush.
- You can take your time with the form.
- I told her to take her time and think about it.

### 6. `get ready`

表示“做好准备”。后接事情时常用 `get ready for ...` 或
`get ready to ...`。

- I need a few minutes to get ready.
- We're getting ready for dinner.
- She got ready to leave the house.

### 7. `get some rest`

表示“休息一下、好好休息”。

- You should go home and get some rest.
- I went to bed early to get some rest.
- Try to get some rest before the trip.

### 8. `have trouble ...-ing`

表示“做某事有困难”，动作使用 `-ing` 形式。

- I have trouble remembering people's names.
- Are you having trouble opening the file?
- She had trouble falling asleep last night.

### 9. `catch the bus / train`

表示“赶上并乘坐公交车或火车”。

- I left early to catch the bus.
- Did you catch the last train?
- We managed to catch the train just in time.

### 10. `miss the bus / train`

表示“没赶上公交车或火车”。

- I missed the bus this morning.
- I don't want to miss the last train.
- Hurry up, or we'll miss the train.

### 11. `run out of ...`

表示“把……用完、没有……了”。

- We ran out of milk.
- We're running out of clean towels.
- We're running out of time.

### 12. `keep in touch`

表示“保持联系”。

- Let's keep in touch.
- We still keep in touch after all these years.
- It's hard to keep in touch when everyone is busy.

### 13. `pick ... up`

表示“取东西、接某人”。代词通常放在 `pick` 和 `up` 中间。

- I need to pick up some groceries on my way home.
- Can you pick me up at the station?
- She picked it up from the front desk.

### 14. `find out ...`

表示“查明、得知”。

- I need to find out when the store closes.
- We found out that the restaurant was full.
- Let me find out what happened.

## 工作交流搭配

### 15. `make progress`

表示“取得进展”。

- We're making good progress on the project.
- I made some progress on the bug yesterday.
- It's difficult to make progress without the test data.

### 16. `meet a deadline`

表示“按时完成，在截止日期前完成”。

- We need to meet the deadline.
- I'm confident that we can meet the deadline.
- The extra help allowed us to meet the deadline.

### 17. `miss a deadline`

表示“错过截止日期、没有按时完成”。

- We may miss the deadline if the issue isn't fixed today.
- I don't want to miss another deadline.
- The team missed the deadline by two days.

### 18. `solve a problem`

表示“解决问题”。

- This change should solve the problem.
- We need more information before we can solve the problem.
- Restarting the service solved the problem temporarily.

### 19. `fix a bug`

表示“修复 Bug”。

- I'm trying to fix a bug in the login flow.
- We fixed the bug before the release.
- This change fixes a bug that affects mobile users.

### 20. `run tests`

表示“运行测试”。

- I need to run the tests again.
- We ran some tests on the new API.
- Please run the tests before you open a pull request.

### 21. `review code`

表示“审查代码”。

- I spent the morning reviewing code.
- Could you review my code when you have time?
- We should review the code before merging it.

### 22. `make a change`

表示“进行修改”。

- I need to make a small change to the configuration.
- We made a few changes after the review.
- Let me know before you make any changes to the API.

### 23. `give feedback`

表示“提供反馈、提出意见”。

- Could you give me some feedback on this design?
- The client gave us useful feedback.
- I'll give you feedback after I test the new version.

### 24. `raise an issue`

表示“提出一个问题，提醒大家注意”。

- I'd like to raise an issue with the current approach.
- She raised the issue during the meeting.
- No one raised any issues with the proposal.

### 25. `take a look at ...`

表示“看一下、检查一下”，比单独使用 `check` 更口语化。

- Could you take a look at this error?
- I'll take a look at the logs after lunch.
- We need someone to take a look at the database.

### 26. `run into a problem`

表示“遇到问题”。

- I ran into a problem during the deployment.
- Let me know if you run into any problems.
- We ran into the same problem last week.

### 27. `come up with a solution`

表示“想出解决方案”。

- We need to come up with a better solution.
- She came up with a simple solution to the problem.
- Let's see if we can come up with a solution today.

### 28. `reach an agreement`

表示“达成一致”。

- We need to reach an agreement before the meeting ends.
- The two teams finally reached an agreement.
- It may take some time to reach an agreement on the details.

### 29. `make sense`

表示“合理、说得通、容易理解”。

- That makes sense to me.
- This part of the requirement doesn't make sense.
- Does it make sense to handle this on the frontend?

## 常见句中和句尾短语

### 30. `at the moment`

表示“目前、此刻”。

- I'm busy at the moment.
- At the moment, we don't have enough information.
- The service isn't available at the moment.

### 31. `for now`

表示“目前暂时这样”，暗示以后可能改变。

- This solution is good enough for now.
- For now, let's keep the current behavior.
- We don't need to change anything for now.

### 32. `by the end of the day`

表示“不晚于今天结束时”。

- I'll send you an update by the end of the day.
- We need to finish this by the end of the day.
- The new version should be ready by the end of the day.

### 33. `as soon as possible`

表示“尽快”。

- Please reply as soon as possible.
- We need to fix the issue as soon as possible.
- I'll get back to you as soon as possible.

### 34. `from time to time`

表示“有时、偶尔”。

- I work from home from time to time.
- We still talk from time to time.
- The app may slow down from time to time.

### 35. `once in a while`

表示“偶尔”，比 `from time to time` 更口语化。

- I eat out once in a while.
- We all need a break once in a while.
- I still watch that show once in a while.

### 36. `at the same time`

表示“同时”，也可以用于补充另一个需要兼顾的观点。

- I can't listen and write at the same time.
- The change makes the page faster, but at the same time, it adds complexity.
- Both requests arrived at the same time.

### 37. `in that case`

表示“既然那样、在那种情况下”。

- In that case, let's wait until tomorrow.
- We don't have access? In that case, we need to ask the admin.
- In that case, the simpler option would be better.

### 38. `in general`

表示“总体来说、一般而言”。

- In general, I prefer working in the morning.
- The new version performs better in general.
- In general, users don't need to change this setting.

### 39. `on my own`

表示“独自、靠自己”。人称可以根据主语变化。

- I learned how to do it on my own.
- I don't think I can finish this on my own.
- She lives on her own.

### 40. `on time`

表示“按时”，即在约定或规定的时间到达或完成。

- The train arrived on time.
- I finished the task on time.
- We need to start the meeting on time.

### 41. `in advance`

表示“提前、预先”。

- Thanks for letting me know in advance.
- You need to book a table in advance.
- We should prepare the test data in advance.

### 42. `in the long run`

表示“从长远来看”。

- This will save us time in the long run.
- The cheaper option may cost more in the long run.
- It will be easier to maintain in the long run.

### 43. `so far`

表示“到目前为止”，常和现在完成时一起使用。

- Everything has gone well so far.
- So far, we haven't found any major issues.
- I've finished three tasks so far.

### 44. `in the meantime`

表示“在此期间、与此同时”。

- The food will take twenty minutes. In the meantime, let's get a drink.
- I'm waiting for their reply. In the meantime, I'll update the tests.
- The fix won't be ready today, so use the old version in the meantime.

### 45. `at least`

表示“至少”，也可以在纠正或补充观点时使用。

- It will take at least two hours.
- At least we know what caused the problem.
- We should test it at least once before the release.
