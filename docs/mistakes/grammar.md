# 语法错误记录

> 只记录真实训练中犯过的语法错误。不系统整理完整语法知识。

## 过去时态

### find 的过去式

- ✗ He finally **founded** the address.
- ✓ He finally **found** the address.

`found` 是 `find` 的过去式，意思是"找到"。`founded` 是 `found` 的过去式，意思是"创立、建立"。

### 复述时时态混用

在复述一段过去的故事时，容易不自觉地切换回现在时：

- ✗ Harry **doesn't** know how to get there.
- ✓ Harry **didn't** know how to get there.

- ✗ They **go** to the restaurant.
- ✓ They **went** to the restaurant.

**提醒**：复述开始前先确认时态基调。如果是在说过去发生的事，全程保持过去时。

## 冠词

### 可数名词单数需要冠词

- ✗ He met **old woman**.
- ✓ He met **an old woman**.

- ✗ They went to **restaurant**.
- ✓ They went to **the restaurant**.

### a 和 an 的选择

元音音素前用 an：

- an old woman
- an hour
- an interesting story

## ask 的句型

### ask someone how to...

- ✗ He asked the woman **for how to** get to the street.
- ✓ He asked the woman **how to** get to the street.

`ask someone how to do something` 中间不需要 `for`。

`ask someone for something` 用于请求具体的东西：

- He asked her **for help**.
- He asked her **for directions**.
