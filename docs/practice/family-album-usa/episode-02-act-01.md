## 暴露的问题

### 表达问题

- 想说"赴约吃晚餐"时不确定怎么表达，用了"make dinner reservation"（实际上是"订位"的意思，不是"赴约"）
- "问路"的表达不稳定，说了"asked...for how to get to"而不是"asked...how to get to"
- 想表达"虽然迟到了但 Susan 仍然很高兴"时卡住，试图说 although 但没说完整
- 想不到"给保姆留电话号码"怎么说，卡了很久

### 语法问题

- 过去时和现在时混用：开头用了 "doesn't know" 而不是 "didn't know"，后面又用了 "go to restaurant" 而不是 "went to the restaurant"
- 冠词缺失："met a old woman" 应该是 "met an old woman"；"go to restaurant" 缺少 "the"
- `ask someone how to...` 结构用错：说了 "asked woman for how to get to" 而不是 "asked the woman how to get to"
- 一个句子塞了太多信息，导致说到一半就乱了

### 词汇问题

- `found` 的过去式说成了 `founded`。`found` 是 `find` 的过去式（找到），`founded` 是 `found` 的过去式（创立）
- `babysitter` 说成了 `baby sister`（保姆 vs 小妹妹）
- 人名 Harry Bennett 被语音识别成各种变体，训练时需要更清晰地发音

### 发音或听力问题

- Wooster Street 的发音不确定
- 人名发音不够清晰，导致语音识别错误较多

### 其他

- 卡住时没有主动降级成简单句，而是停顿或放弃
- 逐字翻译中文导致句子结构不自然

## 本次重点表达

> 本轮最优先训练的 5 个表达标记为 ★

### ★ not know how to get to...

含义：不知道怎么去某个地方

原句：Harry didn't know how to get to 83 Wooster Street.

自己的句子：

- I didn't know how to get to the office.
- She doesn't know how to get to the airport.

### ★ ask someone for help

含义：向某人寻求帮助

原句：He asked a street vendor for help.

自己的句子：

- I asked my colleague for help with the bug.
- She asked the teacher for help.

### ★ ask someone how to get to...

含义：问某人怎么去某个地方

原句：He asked the woman how to get to 83 Wooster Street.

自己的句子：

- I asked him how to get to the station.
- She asked me how to get to the meeting room.

### ★ go the wrong way

含义：走错路

原句：Harry went the wrong way.

自己的句子：

- I went the wrong way and was late for the meeting.
- We went the wrong way because the map was wrong.

### ★ be happy to see someone

含义：很高兴见到某人

原句：Susan was happy to see Harry.

自己的句子：

- I was happy to see my old friend.
- She was happy to see us at the party.

### meet someone for dinner

含义：和某人约好一起吃晚饭

原句：Harry was going to meet Susan for dinner.

自己的句子：

- I'm going to meet my friend for dinner tonight.
- We met our clients for dinner last week.

### give someone directions

含义：给某人指路

原句：The street vendor gave him directions.

自己的句子：

- Can you give me directions to the nearest subway station?
- She gave me directions to the conference room.

### leave the phone number with the babysitter

含义：把电话号码留给保姆

原句：Harry wanted to leave the phone number of the restaurant with the babysitter.

自己的句子：

- I left my phone number with the front desk.
- Please leave your contact information with the receptionist.

## 降级表达示例

想不到复杂句时，主动拆成简单句。

复杂想法：

```text
Although Harry was late, Susan was still happy to see him.
```

简单表达：

```text
Harry was late, but Susan was happy to see him.
```

更简单：

```text
Harry was late.
Susan was still happy to see him.
```

> 能够连续说出正确的简单句，比卡在复杂句上更重要。

- `told him the correct direction` 更自然地说成：`gave him the correct directions`
- `Harry Bennett`、`Wooster Street`、`Marilyn`、`Richard` 等专有名词仍被识别成不同形式。需要结合原始音频才能区分发音问题和语音识别错误。
