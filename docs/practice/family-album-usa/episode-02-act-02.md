## 本次暴露的表达问题

### 1. Thailand restaurant

原表达：

```text
Harry and Susan run to the Thailand restaurant.
```

建议：

```text
Harry and Susan went to a Thai restaurant.
```

- `Thailand` 是国家名，修饰餐厅时使用 `Thai`。
- 复述过去发生的故事时使用 `went`。

### 2. need to care about Harry

原表达：

```text
Because Harry was here the first time, Susan need to care about Harry.
```

建议：

```text
Since it was Harry's first time there, Susan helped him order.
```

`care about Harry` 表示关心 Harry，不等于帮助他点餐。直接说具体动作更清楚。

### 3. didn't care about that

原表达：

```text
Harry didn't care about that.
```

建议：

```text
Harry said he didn't need a menu.
```

`didn't care` 表示不在乎；想表达“不需要”时使用 `didn't need`。

### 4. talking about anything

原表达：

```text
They're talking about anything. About job, about Harry's daughter.
```

建议：

```text
They talked about their jobs and Harry's daughter.
```

这里已经知道具体话题，因此不使用 `anything`。

### 5. a phone call coming

原表达：

```text
There's a phone call coming.
```

建议：

```text
Then Harry got a phone call from the babysitter.
```

### 6. Susan don't care

原表达：

```text
Susan don't care about that. She care about Michelle.
```

建议：

```text
Susan didn't mind. She was worried about Michelle.
```

`didn't mind` 表示 Susan 不介意约会被打断；`was worried about` 表示她担心 Michelle。

### 7. another reservation last time

原表达：

```text
They can make another reservation last time.
```

建议：

```text
They agreed to see each other another time.
```

这里想表达的是以后再约会，不只是重新订餐厅座位。
