## 本次暴露的表达问题

### 1. about Mrs. Tobin and Grandpa by train

原表达：

```text
The story is about Miss Tobin and Grandpa by train.
```

建议：

```text
The story is about Grandpa meeting Mrs. Tobin on a train.
```

`by train` 表示交通方式；描述故事发生地点时使用 `on a train`。

### 2. a woman come to the near train

机器识别出的表达：

```text
A woman come to the near train.
```

想表达她坐到 Grandpa 旁边，可以直接说：

```text
A woman came over and sat next to Grandpa.
```

### 3. helped the woman put her luggage down

机器没有稳定识别 `suitcase`。建议固定为：

```text
Grandpa helped her put her suitcase on the luggage rack.
```

想不起 `luggage rack` 时可以降级为：

```text
Grandpa helped her with her suitcase.
```

### 4. both come from Florida

原表达：

```text
Mrs. Tobin and Grandpa both come from Florida.
Mrs. Tobin live near Orlando, and Grandpa know the area.
```

建议：

```text
They were both from Titusville, Florida, near Orlando.
Grandpa knew the area well.
```

### 5. In the past year Grandpa has a company

原表达：

```text
In the past year Grandpa has a construction company.
```

建议：

```text
Before he retired, Grandpa owned a construction company.
```

这里不是“去年”，而是在说退休以前的职业经历。

### 6. Mrs. Tobin was come to New York

原表达：

```text
Mrs. Tobin was come to New York to meet her friend.
```

建议：

```text
Mrs. Tobin came to New York to visit some close friends.
```

`come` 的一般过去时是 `came`，不使用 `was come`。

### 7. her husband didn't have time

原表达：

```text
Her husband didn't have time to come with her.
```

意思基本清楚，更贴近原剧情的表达是：

```text
Her husband could not take time off to come with her.
He was an aerospace engineer.
```

### 8. checked the ticket because...

原表达的后半句没有形成清楚的因果关系。建议：

```text
The conductor asked for Mrs. Tobin's ticket.
She had changed seats because a man near her was smoking.
```

### 9. have a lot of good memories

原表达：

```text
They have a lot of good memories.
```

建议：

```text
Grandpa had many wonderful memories of his wife.
```

也可以说：

```text
They had shared many wonderful memories.
```

### 10. 两段周年信息

不要把两组数字放在同一句里。固定为：

```text
Grandpa and his wife had been married for forty-seven years.
Mrs. Tobin and her husband would celebrate their fortieth anniversary
the next month.
```

### 11. Grandpa's son and wife and grandchildren

原表达：

```text
Grandpa's son and wife and grandchildren
```

建议：

```text
his son, his son's wife, and their three children
```

这样可以避免让 `wife` 听起来像是 Grandpa 的妻子。

### 12. live New York / be unknown anymore

机器识别出的表达：

```text
Grandpa wanted to leave New York with his family because they didn't
want to be unknown anymore.
```

建议：

```text
Grandpa wanted to live in New York with his family because he did not
want to be alone anymore.
```

- `live in New York` 需要介词 `in`。
- 主语是 Grandpa，后半句使用 `he`。
- `alone` 表示“独自一人”；`unknown` 表示“不为人知的”。

### 语音识别需要谨慎判断的部分

| 机器识别 | 推测的实际表达 |
| -------- | -------------- |
| `Ms. Tobin` / `Mr. Tobin` | `Mrs. Tobin` / `Elsa Tobin` |
| `suitcase rack` | `luggage rack`，意思已经表达清楚 |
| `helped his congestion company` | 可能是 `had his construction company` |
| `this month` / `last month` | 月份识别不一致，正确剧情为 `next month` |

## 第二次复述暴露的表达问题

### 1. helped her to put her suitcase

原表达意思正确，但可以更简洁自然：

```text
Grandpa helped Mrs. Tobin put her suitcase on the luggage rack.
```

`help someone do something` 中间通常不需要 `to`。

### 2. They discovered it. They both from Florida.

原表达：

```text
They discovered it. They both from Florida.
```

建议合并为：

```text
They discovered that they were both from Titusville, Florida.
```

`were` 不能省略；用一个 `that` 从句也能减少句间停顿。

### 3. Titusville, Orlando

`Titusville` 不属于 Orlando，只是靠近 Orlando。建议说：

```text
Titusville is near Orlando.
```

复述过去的对话时也可以说：

```text
They were both from Titusville, a town near Orlando.
```

### 4. live Spaceport

原表达：

```text
Mrs. Tobin and her husband live Spaceport.
```

建议：

```text
Mrs. Tobin and her husband lived near Spaceport.
```

地点前需要 `near`，复述故事时使用过去时 `lived`。

### 5. Grandpa's house only a few miles

原表达缺少谓语：

```text
Grandpa's house only a few miles from Spaceport.
```

建议：

```text
Grandpa's house was only a few miles from Spaceport.
```

### 6. meet her very close friend

原表达：

```text
Mrs. Tobin went to New York to meet her very close friend.
```

剧情中是多位朋友，更自然的说法是：

```text
Mrs. Tobin went to New York to visit some close friends.
```

### 7. They have been married for forty-seven years

Grandpa 的妻子已经去世，因此这里应使用过去完成时：

```text
Grandpa and his wife had been married for forty-seven years.
```

明确说出两个人，也能避免把 47 年误解成 Mrs. Tobin 的婚姻。

### 8. a lot of memory

原表达：

```text
Grandpa had a lot of memory.
```

表示多段具体回忆时，`memory` 使用复数：

```text
Grandpa had many wonderful memories of his wife.
```

### 9. plan to celebrate... this month

建议固定为过去视角下的完整句：

```text
Mrs. Tobin and her husband planned to celebrate their fortieth
anniversary the next month.
```

更接近原对话的转述方式是：

```text
Mrs. Tobin said that she and her husband would celebrate their
fortieth anniversary the next month.
```

### 10. they talk about marriage

原表达：

```text
And then they talk about marriage.
```

建议：

```text
Then they talked about their marriages.
```

这里谈的是两人的婚姻经历，因此复数 `marriages` 更准确。
