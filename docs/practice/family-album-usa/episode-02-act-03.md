## 语音识别需要谨慎判断的部分

以下内容大概率是用户实际说对了、但模型识别失败，或模型在安静段上的幻听，不能据此判断口语错误：

| 机器识别 | 推测的实际表达 |
| -------- | -------------- |
| `she was a male member / female member`（共 4 次） | 反复尝试说 `Marilyn`，全部未被识别；也有幻听可能 |
| `she was a little kid`（共 3 次） | `she brought a little gift`，`gift` 被听成 `kid` |
| `a polo driver` | 可能是 `apologize` |

- `time` 的尾音 `/m/`：被听成 `tie`，说明 `time` 的收尾可能不到位，和 Act 2 里 `didn't care` 这类收尾不清属于同一类问题。

## 本次暴露的表达问题

### 1. They talking about

原表达：

```text
They talking about how we...
```

建议：

```text
Marilyn asked Susan about Harry.
```

过去进行时不能省 `were`；更重要的是，这处想说的内容（Marilyn 问 Susan 对 Harry 的看法）用最简单的句子就能讲清楚。

### 2. they hear somebody was knocking the door

原表达：

```text
In the meantime, they hear somebody was knocking the door.
```

建议：

```text
Suddenly, they heard somebody knocking at the door.
```

- 过去发生的事用 `heard`。
- 惯用搭配是 `knock at the door`，不是 `knock the door`。

### 3. come back to apologize

原表达：

```text
And then come back to apologize for leaving so early.
```

建议：

```text
He came back to apologize for leaving so early.
```

`come` 在这里需要过去式 `came`。这句内容本身是对的，只差时态。

### 4. it was not complaining

原表达：

```text
It was not complaining because the food was delicious.
```

建议：

```text
Marilyn was not complaining, because the food was delicious.
```

`complaining` 的主语是 Marilyn，不是 it。

### 5. the living zone just has a Susan and Harry

原表达：

```text
The living zone just has a Susan and Harry.
```

建议：

```text
After Marilyn left, only Susan and Harry were in the living room.
```

- 人名前不加 `a`。
- `living room` 是房间，`zone` 是区域。
- 独处这个情节点值得单独成句，它是后面深入对话的前提。

### 6. join a fashion show

原表达：

```text
She's going to join a fashion show tomorrow morning.
```

建议：

```text
She's going to take part in a fashion show tomorrow morning.
```

`join` 后面接组织或人群（join a club、join us），参加活动用 `take part in` 或 `be in`。

### 7. Harry needs to go home... Harry has early start

原表达：

```text
Harry needs to go home because the time is midnight. And Harry has
early start tomorrow.
```

建议：

```text
At midnight, Harry had to go home, because he had an early start the
next morning.
```

结尾部分整段滑回现在时。复述越接近结尾时态越容易松，收尾前先在脑子里过一遍"全部用过去时"。

### 8. 结尾句

原表达：

```text
Harry was very happy because she is thinking that they are Susan.
```

建议（对应原剧台词）：

```text
Susan said she would like to meet Michelle. Harry asked, "Does that
mean I can see you again?" Susan said yes, and Harry promised to call
her.
```

## 下一轮训练方法

1. 固定开头两句，不再即兴开场：

```text
This part is about Harry and Susan.
In Act 3, Susan and Marilyn were having dinner at Susan's apartment.
```

2. 用情节关键词先完整讲一遍，目标把总时长压到 3 分钟以内（本次 8 分 30 秒）。
3. 每个情节点只说一个短句，卡住就结束当前句，用 `Suddenly...` / `Then...` / `After that...` 继续。
4. 人名只尝试一次，不行就降级为 `Susan's sister-in-law`。
5. 结尾固定为两句：

```text
Susan said she would like to meet Michelle.
Harry asked, "Does that mean I can see you again?" and promised to call her.
```

6. 目标指标：超过 3 秒的停顿不多于 3 次；全程基本保持过去时。
