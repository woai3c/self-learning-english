## 本次暴露的表达问题

下面保留机器听到的原表达作为练习线索；存疑的词不当作已经确认的口误。优先练能复用的简单句，不追求逐字复现剧情台词。

1. **救人动作的词序**：机器两版都接近 `Robbie pulled out him of the water`。建议固定为 `Robbie pulled him out of the water.`；想不起 `pulled` 时说 `Robbie helped him get out of the water.`。
2. **救治结果**：`Philip was saving him, saving Albert's life` 有重复。复述已经完成的动作时可说 `Philip saved Albert's life.`。
3. **回到父亲身边**：机器识别为 `told Albert to his father`，原音存疑。建议用 `They took Albert to his father.`。
4. **继续钓鱼到医院呼叫**：`we came back to our fishing` 和 `baby mission was screening` 都不稳定。建议连成 `Grandpa wanted to keep fishing, but Philip got a call from the hospital.`；想不起设备名称时直接说 `got a call`。
5. **行程被打断**：`we cannot go into fishing` 不自然。建议说 `They couldn't keep fishing.` 或 `They had to leave.`。
6. **两路人物去向**：`After they got home, Philip had to go to the hospital, and Robbie and Grandpa got home` 先说全体回家、再说 Philip 去医院，容易混乱。建议说 `Philip went to the hospital, while Robbie and Grandpa went home.`。
7. **结尾时间**：`The day we had a good time` 的时间位置不自然，可改为 `They had a good time that day, so they planned to go fishing again next Saturday.`。另一版机器将尾句识别为 `go to fishing`，本版识别为 `go fishing`，是否多说了 `to` 需结合原音确认；练习时固定使用 `go fishing`。

## 想不起准确词时的降级表达

- `pager` / `beeper` → `Philip got a call from the hospital.`
- `pulled Albert out of the water` → `Robbie helped Albert get out of the water.`
- `one of his patients had a high fever` → `Someone at the hospital needed Philip.`
- `apologized for ruining the trip` → `Philip said he was sorry.`
- `became interested in medicine` → `Robbie wanted to learn more about being a doctor.`
